Privacy Policy

Effective 20 October 2026 · applies to DQRS Console and this website

Who we are

Dumb Question Real Science ("DQRS", "we") makes short science videos. DQRS Console is our internal tool for uploading and scheduling our own videos on our own YouTube channel.

We use YouTube API Services

DQRS Console uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What information the tool accesses, collects and stores

  • Google sign-in token. When the channel owner signs in with Google, the tool keeps an OAuth refresh token so it can upload later. It is stored encrypted in the operating system's keychain on the owner's own computer, never in files, logs or code repositories, and never sent anywhere except to Google.
  • Channel identity. The tool reads the signed-in channel's ID and title to make sure it uploads to the right channel. This is not stored.
  • Our own video data. For each video we upload, the tool stores the YouTube video ID, title, scheduled publish time, synthetic-media disclosure and a file checksum in our private project records, and reads back the video's privacy and processing status from YouTube.
  • The tool does not access viewers' data, comments, subscribers, analytics or any other user's account.

How the information is used and shared

The information is used only to upload and schedule our own videos and to confirm what YouTube holds. It is not sold, rented or shared with any internal or external party, and it is not used for advertising or profiling.

Third parties, ads and cookies

  • No third party serves content or ads through DQRS Console or this website.
  • This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process technical request data (such as IP addresses) as described in GitHub's privacy statement.
  • DQRS Console stores nothing on any device other than the owner's own computer (the keychain token and our project records above). It uses no cookies.

Revoking access and deleting data

  • You can revoke DQRS Console's access to a Google account at any time on the Google security settings page. The tool also has its own revoke command, which revokes the token with Google and deletes it from the keychain.
  • To have any stored data deleted, email us at the address below. We delete it within 7 days.
  • Stored video records are kept while the video is on our channel and deleted within 7 days after access is revoked or a deletion request is received.
