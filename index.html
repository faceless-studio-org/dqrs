DQRS Publisher

DQRS Publisher is our own internal tool. It uploads our finished videos to our own YouTube channel and schedules when they go public. It uses YouTube API Services: the YouTube Data API to upload and schedule videos and to check their processing status.

  • It is used only by the channel's owner, for the channel's own videos. It is not offered to other users.
  • It asks for the minimum Google permissions: upload videos (youtube.upload) and read the channel's own video status (youtube.readonly).
  • Nothing is posted without the owner's explicit confirmation, and every upload is checked first (sources in the description, AI/synthetic-media disclosure, publish time).
